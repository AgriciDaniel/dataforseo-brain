# Review: Commerce Scope (Merchant + App Data + Business Data + Databases)

Scope audited: wiki concepts `cap-merchant-api`, `cap-app-data-api`, `cap-business-data-api`, `cap-databases`; platforms `plat-amazon-marketplace`, `plat-google-shopping`, `plat-app-stores`, `plat-review-platforms`. Cross-checked against raw docs under `.raw/sources/dataforseo-research/{merchant,app-data,business-data,databases}/` and live docs at https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/. Reviewer date 2026-06-26.

## Findings

| Severity | Location | Issue | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIGH | `wiki/concepts/cap-app-data-api.md` (params table + Cost notes) and `wiki/platforms/plat-app-stores.md` (Key parameters + Cost notes) | Apple `app_searches` depth/billing is documented as identical to Google Play. cap-app-data row reads "depth \| app_searches default 30, max 200, in multiples of 30 (each 30 = one SERP)"; plat-app-stores reads "depth (Google Play default 30, max 200, billed per SERP of 30; **Apple mirrors this**)" and the cost example "depth: 60 is two SERPs". This is wrong for Apple. Live Apple docs: default `100`, billed per each SERP of up to **100** results, "multiples of 100" (max is far above 200 — docs show 700–1000, not 200). Only Google Play is 30/200/per-30. | Split the depth row by store. Google Play: default 30, max 200, multiples of 30, billed per 30 (correct). Apple: default 100, billed per 100, multiples of 100, max ~700–1000 (confirm exact max). Remove "Apple mirrors this" and fix the "depth:60 = two SERPs" cost example so it is flagged Google-Play-only. Cite https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/app_data/apple/app_searches/task_post/ vs https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/app_data/google/app_searches/task_post/ |
| HIGH | `wiki/concepts/cap-business-data-api.md` (Key parameters row + Gotchas) | Business Listings `location_coordinate` radius unit stated as **meters**: params row says "radius 1-100000 m" and the gotcha says "a 'lat,lng,radius' triple **in meters** (radius 1 to 100000)". Both the raw doc and live docs say the radius is in **kilometres** (1–100,000 km). This is a silent 1000x geographic-scope error that contradicts the brain's own raw source (`business-data/business-listings-search.md`: "radius in km (1-100,000)"). | Change "m"/"meters" to "km"/"kilometres" in both the params row and the gotcha. Cite https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/business_data/business_listings/search/live/ ("the value of 'radius' is specified in kilometres (km)"). |
| MEDIUM | `wiki/platforms/plat-google-shopping.md` (Overview + What it covers + Sources) | Note undercounts Google Shopping Merchant endpoints: Overview says "**Four endpoints** cover the funnel" and "What it covers" lists only products, product_info, sellers, reviews — omitting `merchant/google/product_spec` (Product Specification), which IS documented in `cap-merchant-api`, in the raw (`merchant/google-shopping-product-spec.md` + `_module-index.json`), and live. There are five Google Shopping Merchant endpoints. | Change "Four endpoints" to "Five endpoints" and add a `google/product_spec` bullet (specification attributes by `product_id`). Cite https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/business_data-social_media-overview/ |
| LOW | `wiki/concepts/cap-app-data-api.md` (Cost & method notes) | The `app_listings` per-request cost example cites only "a Google Play example around $0.11 per request"; Apple `app_listings` is a separate Live endpoint with its own (different) rate. Minor — example reads as if it applies to both stores. | Note the $0.11 figure is the Google Play example and Apple `app_listings` is priced separately; or label it "Google Play example". |

## Verdict
Commerce scope is largely accurate, well-structured, and free of broken wikilinks; endpoint coverage for Amazon, app stores, reviews, and databases is solid and database refresh facts (60/90-day SERP/app cycles, monthly keyword, Historical SERP since Aug 2021, Historical Keyword since 2019, 50% refresh pricing, 67-region Unified Search) match live docs. Two HIGH factual errors should be fixed before shipping: the Apple `app_searches` depth/billing (it does NOT mirror Google Play) and the Business Listings radius unit (km, not meters). One MEDIUM coverage gap (missing `google/product_spec` in plat-google-shopping) and two LOW completeness notes round it out.
