## Verdict

The brain is fundamentally solid and close to market-ready: structure passes every hard gate (zero dead wikilinks, 100% frontmatter, full index/MOC coverage, 104 dated citations, a 12-source ledger all future-dated, a healthy canvas), and the load-bearing facts that were sampled across costs, rate limits, error codes, index figures, parameters, and endpoint paths overwhelmingly check out against live docs. There are **no BLOCKERs**. The themes worth acting on are narrow and concentrated: (1) **two genuine correctness errors** that ship wrong numbers - the Business Listings radius unit (wiki says meters, the brain's own raw source and live docs say kilometres, a silent 1000x geo error) and the Apple `app_searches` depth/billing (wiki wrongly says Apple mirrors Google Play's 30/200/per-30; live Apple docs are 100/700/per-100); (2) **staleness on the fast-moving MCP surface** (Node floor is now `>=20.0.0` and the published version is 2.9.9, not 2.7.12); (3) **a handful of coverage gaps** for shipped endpoints the brain markets but omits - SERP `ai_summary`/`screenshot`, the free `backlinks/index`, `google/product_spec`, Lighthouse helper endpoints; and (4) **one cosmetic policy miss** - pervasive em-dashes against the vault's "no em dashes" rule. All four originally-HIGH findings survived re-verification; none were dropped.
