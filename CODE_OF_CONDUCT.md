# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: using welcoming language, respecting
differing viewpoints, accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and focusing on what is best for the
community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting comments, and publishing
others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project owner via the contact in
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
